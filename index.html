About
Based in Greater Seattle Area

I'm Chia-Hung Kuo, a PhD Candidate (ABD) in Economics from Michigan State University, now based in the Greater Seattle Area. I build statistical systems that inform real decisions, currently consulting for an NGO in Taiwan where my analysis directly affects government funding allocations to universities.

My background is in causal inference, econometrics, and experimental design. I care about why things happen, not just what will happen next.

Most recently, I built an uplift modeling and causal decomposition analysis on a 13.9M-user ad campaign, decomposing treatment effects into reachability and responsiveness using instrumental variables.

I also enjoy coding for its own sake. You can find me on LeetCode. Amateur watcher, professional rater on IMDb.
